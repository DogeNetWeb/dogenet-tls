pepenet-tls — fail-closed pages

Every page the DANE proxy can serve in place of a site. Captured from the real proxy over TLS, not mocked up. Open any of them directly; they are self-contained (no external CSS, fonts or images).

ERROR 502
Origin key does not match the chain
The security case. The origin answered, but presented a key its owner never pinned. Expand Diagnostics to see the pinned TLSA and the SPKI the origin actually served — the two hashes differ, which is the whole point.
 ERROR 504
Origin unreachable
Name resolved, nothing answered at the published address. Site offline, or a stale A record.
 ERROR 404
No such name on the chain
Nothing published for this name: never registered, lease lapsed, or no records added yet. No origin was dialled.

Regenerate after editing serve_error() in src/proxy.c: these were produced by driving the real proxy_serve() against a local origin whose certificate deliberately mismatches its TLSA. Toggle your OS between light and dark appearance to check both themes — the pages follow prefers-color-scheme.
